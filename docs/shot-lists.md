# QSC named shot lists — draft for review

Replaces "Add photo × N" with named slots. One tap per slot, camera only.

**How to read this:** `★` marks the three I'd put in the default rotation for that
section — the ones that earn their place on flag history. The rest fill in on later
visits. A spot check asks for 3 shots in each of its 2 sections: six photos, same as
today, every one of them specified.

**Why these shots:** every one names a surface that doesn't get cleaned unless
someone looks — undersides, wheels, thresholds, the wall-to-floor junction, the
corner away from the door. A photo of the front of a fryer proves nothing; the floor
behind it is the whole question.

Flag counts are from every audit on record, and are why the lists are uneven —
Kitchen & BOH has drawn 60 flags, Health Dept Readiness one.
